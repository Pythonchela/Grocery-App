## Full description (max 4000)

BazaarX brings products from many sellers into one place.

- Browse categories, search, and compare products with photos, details and reviews.
- Add to your cart and check out with the payment options the store offers.
- Follow every order from confirmation to delivery, with live tracking.
- Ask for a return or a refund from the same screen.
- Save favourites to your wishlist and get notified about your orders.
- Earn and use rewards and referral credit where the store offers them.

Want to sell? Get the BazaarX Seller app.
